Superstore Sales Analysis Report¶

Executive Summary¶

This analysis examined 9,994 orders across 4 years (2014–2017). Total revenue: $2.3M | Total profit: $286K | Avg margin: 12.5%

Key Findings¶

  1. Technology drives highest revenue but Furniture has thin margins
  2. West region leads in sales; Central lags behind
  3. Discounts above 20% consistently produce negative profit
  4. Q4 is consistently the strongest quarter (holiday effect)

Recommendations¶

  • Reduce discounts on Furniture — they erode margin without boosting volume
  • Investigate Tables sub-category (consistent losses every year)
  • Prioritize Corporate segment — highest average order value
